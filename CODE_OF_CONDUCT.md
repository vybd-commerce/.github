# Code of Conduct

Vybd projects follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and considerate. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing of others' private information.
- Assume good intent, and accept correction gracefully when you get something wrong.

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and wherever someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **hello@vybd.ai**. Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code, and may ban contributors temporarily or permanently.
